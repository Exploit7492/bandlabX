## Provenance

This client was generated with [Stainless](https://www.stainless.com/) and then extended. The
endpoint surface was reconstructed from public and reverse-engineered sources (the published
OpenAPI spec, the BandLab web application bundle, and a decompiled build of the BandLab Android
app). See `docs/research/` and `docs/handover/` for the method and its limits. Endpoint knowledge
is reconstructed, not provided by BandLab, and is not warranted to be current or complete.
